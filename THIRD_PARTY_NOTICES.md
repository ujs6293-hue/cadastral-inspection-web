# Third-party components

All dependencies are self-hosted; no runtime third-party document processing service is used.

- PDF.js / pdfjs-dist 4.10.38: Apache-2.0. https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js . License in docs/vendor/pdfjs/LICENSE; standard font notices in standard_fonts.
- Tesseract.js 6.0.1 and Tesseract.js-core 6.0.0: Apache-2.0. https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js and https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js-core . Bundled license files in their respective directories.
- Korean and English traineddata, @tesseract.js-data 1.0.0, 4.0.0_best_int: public Tesseract OCR language data. Apache-2.0. https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata . License in docs/vendor/tessdata/LICENSE.
- fflate 0.8.2: MIT. https://github.com/101arrowz/fflate . License in docs/vendor/fflate-LICENSE.

Exact asset download locations and hashes are recorded in docs/vendor/ASSETS.json. Original private manuals, samples, reports and extracted user content are not included.
